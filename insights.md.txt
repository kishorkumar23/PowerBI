# Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Road Transportation Is a Key Delay Driver

**Insight:**

Road shipments represent a significant share of overall shipment volume and record the highest delay rate among transportation modes. This indicates that road-based deliveries are a key area for operational improvement, with opportunities to optimize routing, carrier performance, and delivery scheduling.

**Business Impact:**
- Higher delay exposure for road shipments
- Potential increase in customer complaints and support requests
- Opportunity to improve transportation reliability

**Recommendation:**

Prioritize route optimization, carrier performance monitoring, and proactive scheduling for road shipments, particularly for high-importance products.

---

## 2. Delivery Delays Increase Customer Support Demand

**Insight:**

Delayed shipments generate an average of **4.15 customer-care calls per shipment**, compared with **3.99 calls** for on-time shipments. This represents approximately **4.0% higher support demand** for delayed shipments.

**Business Impact:**
- Delayed deliveries create additional customer-service workload
- Higher support interactions increase operational effort
- Reducing delivery delays can help lower avoidable support demand

**Recommendation:**

Implement proactive customer notifications and delay alerts to identify and communicate potential delivery issues before customers need to contact support.

---

## 3. Discount Level Has Limited Impact on Customer Rating

**Insight:**

Customer ratings remain relatively stable across discount levels:

| Discount Level | Average Customer Rating |
|---|---:|
| Low (1–2%) | 3.02 |
| Medium (3–5%) | 2.96 |
| High (6–10%) | 2.99 |

The maximum variation is only **0.06 rating points**, suggesting that discount level alone has limited impact on customer satisfaction.

**Business Impact:**
- Increasing discounts does not appear to significantly improve customer ratings
- Customer satisfaction may depend more strongly on other factors such as delivery experience and service quality
- Excessive discounting may not provide sufficient customer-experience benefits

**Recommendation:**

Use a data-driven discount strategy and evaluate promotional decisions alongside delivery performance and customer satisfaction rather than relying on discounts alone.

---

## 4. Warehouse Performance Varies Across Locations

**Insight:**

Delivery performance varies across warehouse locations, indicating that warehouse-level processes may contribute to differences in shipment delays.

**Business Impact:**
- Underperforming warehouses may contribute disproportionately to overall delays
- Differences in processing, handling, staffing, or dispatch efficiency may affect delivery performance
- Warehouse-level analysis can identify specific operational bottlenecks

**Recommendation:**

Prioritize the highest-delay warehouse for root-cause analysis. Review processing time, shipment volume, staffing, inventory handling, and dispatch efficiency to identify corrective actions.

---

## 5. High-Priority Shipments Require Proactive Delivery Management

**Insight:**

High-importance shipments require greater delivery reliability because delays can have a larger operational and customer impact. The analysis highlights the need for priority-based delivery management.

**Business Impact:**
- Delays in critical shipments can have greater consequences
- High-priority orders require better monitoring and faster intervention
- Prioritization can help reduce the risk associated with critical deliveries

**Recommendation:**

Prioritize high-importance shipments through reliable transportation modes, proactive monitoring, and improved dispatch planning. Introduce early-warning alerts for high-risk shipments to enable timely intervention.

---

# Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Optimize Transportation

Prioritize faster and more reliable transportation modes for high-importance shipments, especially where specific modes demonstrate higher delay rates.

## 2. Improve Warehouse Operations

Conduct a performance audit of the highest-delay warehouse and identify operational bottlenecks in processing, handling, and dispatch.

## 3. Optimize Discount Strategy

Adopt data-driven discount thresholds and continuously monitor their relationship with customer satisfaction and delivery performance.

## 4. Reduce Customer Support Workload

Implement proactive notifications and delay alerts to reduce avoidable customer-care interactions caused by delayed shipments.

## 5. Prioritize High-Importance Shipments

Use shipment importance and historical delay patterns to prioritize critical shipments for reliable transportation and proactive monitoring.

---

# Executive Takeaway

Delivery performance is influenced by transportation reliability, warehouse operations, and customer-service demand. The analysis indicates that reducing shipment delays should remain the primary operational objective, supported by targeted transportation optimization, warehouse-level intervention, proactive customer communication, and data-driven promotional strategies.

By focusing on these areas, the organization can improve on-time delivery performance, reduce operational support workload, and strengthen the overall customer experience.
